# Business Insights

## 1. Coffee contains the highest caffeine.

Coffee has an average caffeine value of 293.75 mg, which is
significantly higher than all other beverage categories.

## 2. Smoothies and Frappuccino beverages contain the highest calories.

Some smoothie and Frappuccino categories exceed 250 calories on average.

## 3. The average beverage contains 194 calories.

Customers looking for low-calorie options may prefer coffee or lighter
beverages.

## 4. Average sugar content is high.

The average sugar value across beverages is 33 grams, indicating that
many drinks contain large amounts of sugar.

## 5. Espresso drinks provide high caffeine with fewer calories.

Classic Espresso delivers high caffeine while maintaining relatively low
calorie values.

## 6. Beverage categories are evenly distributed.

Frappuccino and espresso-based beverages form a large portion of the
menu distribution.

## 7. Iced beverages provide moderate caffeine levels.

Iced beverages average around 98.53 mg of caffeine, making them a
balanced choice for customers.

## 8. Protein filters can help customers select healthier drinks.

The dashboard allows users to identify beverages based on nutritional
preferences.
